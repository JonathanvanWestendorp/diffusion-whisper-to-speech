# Whisper to Speech Conversion Using Diffusion Models

MSc Artificial Intelligence thesis, University of Amsterdam (June 2022), by Jonathan van Westendorp. Full text: [final.pdf](final.pdf).

## Abstract

Whispering is often the only way to communicate without problems for people who stutter or suffer from vocal cord impairment. This group of people could greatly benefit from a whisper to speech conversion model that gives them back their own natural voice. We propose a novel approach to whisper to speech conversion, based on diffusion models. Diffusion models do not require adversarial training, making them more stable than generative adversarial networks. They can also sample from the true posterior with high fidelity through a process called Langevin Dynamics. We show that our diffusion-based method achieves intelligibility that is highly competitive with the state of the art solution employed by Whispp, while being superior in terms of naturalness. We conclude by presenting our method in a streaming setting, paving the way for future inference speed optimization.
